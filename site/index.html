Super Cub

Your network is your net worth. Super Cub builds it while you study.

three taps

Scan to text Cubbie

Opens a text to Cubbie with the install message already typed.

Tap to text Cubbie instead
  1. Send the text. Cubbie asks before it fetches anything. Reply yes.
  2. One more yes. Cubbie sends the defaults in one message. Say yes, or tell it what to change.
  3. Live your week. Every 2 hours Cubbie syncs a few facts about your classes and texts you only when it finds someone worth meeting. You both say yes before anything is shared.

what it notices

Your Cubbie already knows your classes, deadlines, calendar and what you've told it you're into. Super Cub puts that picture next to everyone else's and looks for the overlaps you'd never see yourself.

Someone in your section stuck on the same assignment this week. A teammate for the hackathon you both registered for. A classmate who already aced the thing you're learning. A ride to the same airport Friday, dinner at the same event, a gym buddy, a project that needs exactly what you know.

You can also just ask. "Stuck on hash tables in CSE 310" or "need a frontend person for the hackathon" and it looks right away.

It learns you as you go, so the suggestions get sharper, and every student who joins makes it better for everyone else. It introduces you only once you both say yes, and it never shares grades, emails, messages or private calendar titles.

Text leave super cub any time and everything about you is deleted. New to Cubbie? The same text starts your Cubbie account first.
