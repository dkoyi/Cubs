a Cubbie Skill

Super Cub

Cubbie understands your life. Super Cub discovers where it intersects with others.

three taps

Copy, then text it to Cubbie

Paste this into your Cubbie chat. Every Cubbie has its own number, so use the chat you already have.

Install this skill: https://github.com/dkoyi/Cubs/tree/main/supercub
  1. Copy the message and text it to your Cubbie. Cubbie asks before it fetches anything. Reply yes.
  2. One more yes. Cubbie sends the defaults in one message. Say yes, or tell it what to change.
  3. Live your week. You never submit a request. Every 2 hours Cubbie syncs a few approved facts about your classes and what you've told it you're up to, and texts you only when it finds someone worth meeting. You both say yes before anything is shared.

what it notices

Your Cubbie already knows your classes, deadlines and calendar. More than that, it knows what you've told it over time: what you're learning, what you're building, what you're stuck on, what you're into and what you're trying to get to. Super Cub turns that into a few short signals, puts them next to everyone else's and looks for the overlaps you'd never see yourself.

Someone in your section stuck on the same assignment this week. A teammate for the hackathon you both registered for. A classmate who told their Cubbie they'd happily help with the thing you're learning. A ride to the same airport Friday, dinner at the same event, a project that needs exactly what you know.

Nobody fills out a profile or posts a request. The network notices on its own. If you want something now, you can still just ask: "Stuck on hash tables in CSE 310" or "need a frontend person for the hackathon".

What you tell Cubbie over time is the heart of it. Super Cub uses what those conversations reveal, never the conversations themselves. No chat history, email or calendar entry ever leaves your Cubbie, only short facts like "working on CSE 240 Project 1". It never shares grades, and it only says you can help with something if you said so yourself. It introduces you only once you both say yes, and every student who joins makes it better for everyone else.

Text leave super cub any time and everything about you is deleted. New to Cubbie? Get your Cubbie at cubbie.ai first, then send it the text.
